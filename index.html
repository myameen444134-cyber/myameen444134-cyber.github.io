About the Game

A Local Duel, Reimagined

Soul Clash: Realm Duel is a fast-paced, local 2-player reflex game built entirely for a single mobile screen. Lay your phone flat on a table, and the display splits cleanly in half — one side rotated 180 degrees so it reads right-side up for whoever is sitting opposite you. No second device, no Wi-Fi, no accounts. Just two players, one screen, and thirty seconds (or however long you choose) of pure reflex combat.

One player commands the glowing, gold-trimmed Human Realm; the other channels the fiery Demon Realm. Both realms are filled with drifting Soul Orbs worth points, rare Golden Relics worth a big bonus, and Cursed Skulls that punish a careless tap. Whoever collects the most souls before the timer hits zero claims victory.

How to Play

Know Your Realm

  1. 1Lay your phone flat on a table between two players, and open the game.
  2. 2Pick a round length on the Start Screen, from a quick 10-second sprint to a full 60-second duel.
  3. 3Human Realm (top half) and Demon Realm (bottom half) each get their own half of the screen — tap or swipe glowing Soul Orbs in your half to score.
  4. 4Grab a Golden Relic for a big +10 bonus — but watch out for the Cursed Skull, which costs you -5.
  5. 5When the timer hits zero, whoever has the most points wins the realm. Tap Play Again for an instant rematch.
